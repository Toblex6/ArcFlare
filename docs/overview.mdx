FlareHQ (`flarehq.xyz`) is an API-first payment protocol designed for digital businesses and autonomous AI agents. Settled on **Arc testnet (`eip155:5042002`)**, FlareHQ provides developer-friendly APIs modeled after modern payment providers to handle USDC checkouts, HTTP 402 micro-monetization, payment streams, and programmatic escrows.
