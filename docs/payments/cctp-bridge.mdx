> **Status: testnet / development only.** CCTP bridging is live on **Arc Testnet** only.
> Verified end-to-end testnet transfers exist from Ethereum Sepolia, Base Sepolia, and
> Optimism Sepolia into Arc Testnet (September 2026, mint transactions recorded on
> chain `5042002`). **Arc Mainnet CCTP is not live**: no Arc Mainnet inbound or outbound
> CCTP transfer has been completed, and the Mainnet integration is fail-closed until
> its configuration is verified. Do not treat any testnet bridge transfer as proof of
> Arc Mainnet support.

Bridge native USDC from Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, or Polygon testnets into Arc Testnet (`5042002`) with 1:1 capital efficiency.
