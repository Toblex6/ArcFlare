Lock funds until project deliverables pass validation checks.

> **Status: Arc Testnet.** The `FlareHQEscrow` contract is deployed and
> production-used on **Arc Testnet** (`0xEb810aeD24D2314dB7471E44bf6DE89f017631E0` —
> escrow creation, release, dispute, and refund transactions on chain `5042002`).
> There is **no Arc Mainnet escrow deployment**; the only configured address is the
> Arc Testnet deployment. Escrow must not be offered on Arc Mainnet until a Mainnet
> deployment exists and is wired per-network.

---

## Initiate Escrow

```typescript
const escrow = await flarehq.escrow.create({
  amount: '200.00',
  provider: '0xWorkerAddress',
  arbiter: '0xArbiterAddress',
  releaseDeadline: 1770000000,
});
```
